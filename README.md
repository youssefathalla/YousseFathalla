### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Front-End Angular Developer with 3+ years of experience** specializing in the modern Angular ecosystem (**v16–v22+**). I focus on engineering high-performance, accessible, enterprise-grade applications using **Standalone Architecture, Angular Signals, SignalStore, and Reactive patterns**.

I actively pioneer **AI-augmented development workflows**, leveraging next-generation AI IDEs (**Kiro, Cursor, Antigravity**) alongside custom AI steering rules (`.kiro`, `.agents`, `.cursorrules`) and architectural context anchors to deliver clean, maintainable, and battle-tested codebases.

- 🔭 **Currently Engineering:** Tier-1 corporate banking platforms at **eSpace** for major financial institutions in the GCC region.
- ⚡ **Core Philosophy:** Direct, signal-first reactivity, strict type safety, zero unnecessary re-renders, and WCAG-accessible UI design.
- 🌍 **Bilingual Systems:** Expert in dynamic internationalization (i18n) and bidirectional (RTL / LTR) layouts for Arabic and English.
- 💬 **Ask Me About:** Modern Angular (v17–v22+), Zoneless architecture, Signals & SignalStore, Tailwind CSS v4, and AI-assisted workflows.
